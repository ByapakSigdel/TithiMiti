## Full description (4000 chars max)

TithiMiti is a beautifully designed Nepali calendar (Bikram Sambat patro) by Hundred Studios — everything you need for Nepali dates in one fast, clean app.

**Calendar**
• Full Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar with English (AD) dates side by side
• Switch instantly between BS and AD month views
• Tithi shown for every day, with festivals and public holidays highlighted
• Detailed daily panchang: sunrise/sunset, moonrise/moonset, nakshatra, yog, karan, pakshya, rashi, ritu, and auspicious muhurats
• Nepal Sambat and Sak Sambat years
• Works offline after the first load — months you've viewed stay available

**Your events**
• Add personal events and get reminder notifications
• See this month's festivals and your events together in one list

**Tools**
• Live gold & silver prices (tola and 10g) from trusted Nepali sources
• Currency exchange rates for major currencies against NPR, with a two-way converter
• AD ↔ BS date converter
• Daily horoscope (rashifal) for all 12 signs

**Home screen widgets**
• Today's BS date with tithi and sun times
• Upcoming event at a glance
• Gold & silver prices
• Daily horoscope with rotating artwork

**Designed by Hundred Studios**
A warm, distinctive design in light and dark themes — a Nepali calendar that's a pleasure to check every day.

No account. No ads. No tracking.
