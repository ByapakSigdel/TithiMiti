# Privacy Policy — TithiMiti

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026
**Developer:** Hundred Studios

TithiMiti ("the app") is a Nepali calendar app that shows Bikram Sambat dates, tithi and panchang details, festivals and events, gold/silver prices, currency exchange rates, and a daily horoscope.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

- The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no registration.
- The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We never receive any information about you or your device.

## Data stored on your device

The following data stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us:

- Events and reminders you create.
- Your selected zodiac sign and theme preference.
- Cached calendar, price, and horoscope data (kept locally so the app works offline).

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Network requests

To show up-to-date information, the app fetches publicly available data from these sources:

- **Calendar data:** cal.mahansigdel.com.np and hamropatro.com (Bikram Sambat dates, tithi, panchang, festivals)
- **Gold/silver prices:** hamropatro.com and ashesh.com.np
- **Currency exchange rates:** Nepal Rastra Bank and open exchange-rate APIs
- **Artwork:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art open-access API (horoscope illustrations)

These are ordinary HTTP requests for public data. They include no personal information beyond what any web request technically carries (such as an IP address, visible to the source server as with any internet connection). We do not operate any of these services and do not receive anything from them about you.

## Notifications

Event reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — to fetch calendar, price, and exchange-rate data.
- **Notifications** — to show reminders for events you create.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
